3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Processing your quote request and replying to you — legal basis: pre-contractual steps taken at your request, and your consent given via the form's checkbox.
  • Performing the transport service if the quote is accepted — legal basis: performance of the contract.
  • Meeting our legal and accounting obligations — legal basis: legal obligation.

Your data is not used for any marketing purpose without your prior agreement, and is never sold or rented to third parties.

4. Recipients

Your data is accessible only to HBAS TRANSPORT staff responsible for handling requests and operations. It may be shared with :

  • our hosting and email provider, for technical purposes only ;
  • where applicable, a partner carrier strictly necessary to perform the job ;
  • the competent authorities where the law requires it.

5. Retention period

  • Request with no follow-up: 12 months from the last contact.
  • Client (quote accepted): for the duration of the business relationship, then retention of accounting documents for the statutory period applicable in Belgium.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights :

  • right of access to your data ;
  • right to rectification ;
  • right to erasure ;
  • right to restriction of processing ;
  • right to object ;
  • right to data portability ;
  • right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, write to us at service@hbastransport.com. We reply within one month at the latest.

You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (DPA), Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels — autoriteprotectiondonnees.be.

7. Cookies

This site sets no advertising or behavioural tracking cookies. Only cookies strictly necessary for the site to work may be used.

The Google Maps location map is not loaded automatically. It only appears if you click "Show the map". Until you do, no data is sent to Google. If you click, your IP address and browsing data are sent to Google in accordance with its own privacy policy.
