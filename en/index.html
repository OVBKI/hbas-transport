Our services

A transport solution for every need.

One-off or recurring, urgent or planned, national or international: we match the vehicle, the loading method and the lead time to your goods.

National transport

Deliveries throughout Belgium, direct or multi-drop, with deadlines kept.

International transport

Shipments to and from Belgium across Europe, depending on destination and your customs requirements.

Express delivery

Priority solutions for urgent jobs: immediate departure, direct route, fastest possible delivery.

Dedicated transport

A vehicle reserved exclusively for your goods, with no grouping or transhipment.

Direct delivery A → B

From loading point to delivery point, with no break of load and no warehouse stop.

Full loads (FTL)

The whole truck for a single client: maximum capacity, direct route, goods never unloaded.

Partial loads (LTL)

You only pay for the space you use: ideal for partial volumes and regular shipments.

Pallet transport

Euro pallets and custom pallets, strapped and secured, from a single pallet to a full load.

Distribution

Warehouses, logistics platforms, shops and businesses: rounds and multi-client deliveries.

Frequently asked questions

You are probably asking these questions.

Do you deliver throughout Belgium?

Yes. We deliver throughout Belgium — Flanders, Wallonia and Brussels.

Do you handle international transport?

Yes, depending on the destination. Send us the loading and delivery locations and we will quickly confirm feasibility and lead time.

Can I request a quote free of charge?

Yes, the quote is free and without obligation. Fill in the form or call us and we will come back with a clear proposal.

What types of goods do you carry?

Pallets, general goods, parcels, industrial equipment and other permitted goods.

How can I track my delivery?

We keep our clients informed of progress, from loading through to delivery confirmation.

What are your lead times?

They depend on the destination and how urgent the request is. For urgent needs, our express service allows departure as soon as possible.
